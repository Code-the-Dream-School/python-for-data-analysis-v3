# Lesson 10 — Final Project I: Pipeline and Analysis

**Lesson Overview**

The final project spans two weeks, and this is Phase 1. In a Kaggle notebook, you will take the dataset you chose in your Week 5 proposal and turn it into a complete analysis: load it, clean it, analyze it, visualize the results, and write up your findings in a `summary.md`. Next week, Phase 2 builds an interactive dashboard on top of this work, so a solid analysis this week sets up a smooth second week.

You are not starting cold. Your project repository was created with the Week 5 proposal, and your Week 8 milestone already loaded the data into SQLite and ran analytical queries against it. This week brings those threads together into a finished pipeline.

**Before you begin, read the [Final Project Overview](https://github.com/Code-the-Dream-School/python-for-data-analysis-v3/blob/main/resources/final_project_overview.md)** for the full two-week arc, the dataset options, and the rubric your work is graded against. The Phase 1 rubric is what this week is evaluated on.

**Learning Objectives**

This week, I can...

* Sharpen my project into a specific, answerable question and plan my notebook.
* Load and clean my dataset, documenting each cleaning decision.
* Analyze the data with aggregations, derived features, and my Week 8 SQL queries.
* Support at least three findings with clear charts, and write them up in `summary.md`.

## Topics

1. **[Scoping Your Analysis](<10 - Final Project I/01_scoping.md>)**

   Revisiting your proposal, sharpening your question, deciding what counts as an insight, and planning the structure of your notebook.

2. **[Loading and Cleaning Your Dataset](<10 - Final Project I/02_loading_cleaning.md>)**

   Loading your dataset and applying the Week 3 cleaning tools, with every decision documented in markdown and the cleaned data saved for Phase 2.

3. **[Analysis and Visualization](<10 - Final Project I/03_analysis_visualization.md>)**

   Answering your question with aggregations, derived features, and your Week 8 queries, and supporting each finding with a clear, well-labeled chart.

4. **[Writing Up Your Findings](<10 - Final Project I/04_writeup.md>)**

   Writing `summary.md` — the question, the data, the findings, and the limitations — which also becomes the script for your Week 11 presentation.

## Submitting This Week

This week has two submissions: your **Kaggle notebook** (the analysis) and a **pull request** to your project repository containing `summary.md` and your cleaned data. The requirements and the Phase 1 rubric are on the [assignment page](../assignments/10 - Final Project I.md).
